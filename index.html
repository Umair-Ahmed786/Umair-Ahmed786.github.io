About

I'm a Full-Stack AI Engineer at Revonix. I work across React and Next.js frontends, Node.js backends and Python model services, and I care about AI features that hold up in production, not just in a demo.

On Adroom, a generative-AI ad platform, I designed the quality gate: every AI-generated video is scored against Google's ABCD rubric, weak results are regenerated, and if the scorer itself fails, the video still ships and the error is logged.

Before Revonix I was a Software Engineer at Sukkur IBA University, building web apps for 500+ users and government records systems for Sindh. I also build and look after Shopify and WordPress sites for UK agencies.

PakLegalAid

View the source code

An AI legal assistant for Pakistani law, and my final-year project. I collected two datasets, fine-tuned DistilBERT to tell legal questions from everything else (92%+ accuracy), adapted LLaMA 3.2 with LoRA and QLoRA, and grounded every answer in Pakistani legal documents through a retrieval pipeline, served by FastAPI with a streaming chat interface.
